Connection & configuration

Authorize your Discord account, tell the Worker which Nintendo account to watch, and your Switch status shows on Discord automatically — even with this page closed.

You're not signed in. Press Authorize Discord App below to get started. Settings can only be saved to your account after you authorize — until then you can fill things in and they'll wait on this device.
Where do I find my NSA ID? Status comes from the free nxapi presence server. Go to nxapi-auth.fancy.org.uk, sign in, enter your friend code and accept the friend request from the presence server. The presence URL it shows you ends with your NSA ID — a code like 644cd5195d154bd5.
What's stored, and where: your Discord authorization is kept privately in this Worker's database so your status keeps updating while your browser is closed. Everything is saved under your own Discord account — other people can't see or change it. Discord's permission screen lists everything the sdk.social_layer_presence scope includes (it is broader than just presence). Use Disconnect Discord & delete my data any time to revoke it and erase everything stored for you.

Owner setup checklist

Only the person running the Worker needs this.

In Cloudflare: a KV binding named CODYRPC_CONFIG, a secret CODYRPC_TOKEN (the admin token — keep it to yourself), and a cron trigger * * * * *. Optional variables: MAX_USERS (default 6 — the free plan allows about 6 people at 15-second checks) and ALLOWED_USERS (comma-separated Discord user IDs; if set, only they can sign up). In the Discord Developer Portal, add this page's exact URL as a Redirect URI and keep the app a Public Client.

Admin

For the owner only. Enter the Worker's CODYRPC_TOKEN to see who is linked and remove people. Normal users never need this.
